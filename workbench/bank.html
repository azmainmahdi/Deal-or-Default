Bank: three Deco looks, top-left of the board

A. Marble and gold

Today's white marble bank, made Deco: stepped gold crown with a sunburst, fluted columns, black stepped base.

B. Black lacquer tower

A stepped Deco skyscraper in the black lacquer and gold of the die and gavel. Coins drop into the crown.

C. Vault seal

A round gold vault door with a sunburst and a spoked wheel, pinned over the frame's corner like a seal.
