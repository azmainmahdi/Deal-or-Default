Borrow and repay (debt rule C)

Same moment in each: Ana, 14 gold, owes a 2-coin loan from 2 turns ago. Repay it next turn or sooner for +1, later +2.

A. Picker and loan pills

Borrow opens a picker like Invest. Each loan is a silver pill: size, then turns left at the +1 rate.
