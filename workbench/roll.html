Start of a turn: the die and the bank

Ana, 14 gold, owes a 2-coin loan. The player row never changes: the money line stays the main thing. Pick where the die and the bank live.

A. Tap the bank, die beside the panel

The marble bank on the table is the bank: tap it to borrow or repay. A silver badge shows what you owe. The die sits under the players panel; tap it to roll.
