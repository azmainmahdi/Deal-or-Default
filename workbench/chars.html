Character cards in the game

A. Card rows

The players panel keeps its layout, but each avatar circle becomes a small copy of the printed card. Least change, works on phones.

B. Turn spotlight

Whoever's turn it is gets their full card in the roll box, next to the die. Everyone else is a small card in a strip below, so you always see whose turn it is from the art.

C. Seat cards on the table

Each player's card sits on the table beside the board, like the physical game, with money on a badge across the bottom. The active card lifts and glows. Replaces the avatars in the players panel.

Setup (goes with any of the three): pick your card

Instead of a country dropdown, the six cards are laid out. Click one to take it; taken cards go grey. Hovering lifts the card and shows its perk.
